## **2. Legal Basis for Processing**

The processing of personal data requires a legal basis, which we outline below.

Where the processing of personal data is necessary for the performance of a contract to which the data subject is a party, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(b) GDPR. This also applies to processing operations necessary to carry out pre-contractual measures.

Where processing is necessary for the purposes of a legitimate interest pursued by our company or a third party, and where such interests are not overridden by the interests or fundamental rights and freedoms of the data subject, the legal basis is Article 6(1)(f) GDPR. The legitimate interest of our company lies in the performance of our business operations and in the analysis, optimization, and security of our online offering.

## **4. Use of Data**

Widgetbook GmbH uses the collected data for the following purposes:

- To provide and maintain our service
- To notify you about changes to our service
- To enable you to participate in interactive features of our service, should you choose to do so
- To provide customer support
- To gather analysis or valuable information to improve our service
- To monitor the usage of our service
- To detect, prevent, and resolve technical issues
- To send you messages, special offers, and general information about goods, services, and events we offer that are similar to those you have already purchased or requested, unless you have opted out of receiving such information
- To identify and contact organizations and professionals who may benefit from Widgetbook, based on publicly available information (see Section 4a)
- To help registered users get started with Widgetbook Cloud and to inform them about plans that fit their usage (see Section 4b)

### **4a. Data from Public Sources**

To find organizations that may benefit from Widgetbook, we collect business information from publicly available sources. This includes public code repositories and package registries (e.g. GitHub, pub.dev), company websites, job postings, conference talks and professional networks (e.g. LinkedIn).

**Data categories:** company name and domain, publicly visible use of Flutter or Widgetbook, and for individual contacts: name, job title, employer, public professional profile, and an email address only where the person has published it themselves (for example on a personal website, talk slides or a blog).

**Purpose:** identifying and prioritizing potential customers and contacting them once with a relevant, personalized message. Our first contact is through a professional network or community, not by email. We use a published email address only after the person has accepted our contact request or replied.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is direct marketing of our products to businesses. We only use professional, publicly available information and contact people in their professional role.

**Information:** If we process your data from public sources, we inform you at the latest with our first message, which links to this policy and lets you object at any time.

### **4b. Onboarding and Conversion of Registered Users**

When you register for Widgetbook Cloud, we use your registration data (see Section 7) and information about how you and your organization use Widgetbook Cloud to help you get started and to suggest plans that fit your usage. This includes your name, email address, company, role in the workspace (for example owner or member), job title (if you provided one), sign-up date, setup progress (projects created, repositories connected, teammates invited), builds and snapshots including whether builds failed and which CI tool ran them, pull requests with UI changes and their review status, whether you viewed, reviewed or commented on changes (counts only), whether Figma is connected, plan, usage against plan limits and last activity. We use this to decide whom in a workspace to contact and with which offer, for example when setup stalls, builds keep failing, UI changes wait for review, invited teammates have not joined, or a team is close to a plan limit.

We only use activity counts and statuses for this. We do not analyse the content of your source code, uploaded builds, snapshots, build logs, pull request titles, comments or reviews for this purpose.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR for onboarding messages needed to use the service. Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR for suggestions about plans and features, based on our legitimate interest in offering relevant products to our users. Promotional emails are only sent where permitted by law or with your consent, and you can unsubscribe at any time.

### **4c. AI-Assisted Processing**

We use AI tools to analyze the data described in Sections 4a, 4b and 4d and to prepare draft messages. Our provider is Anthropic (Claude). Anthropic acts as our processor under a data processing agreement and does not use this data to train its models.

Every message is reviewed and sent by a member of our team. We do not make decisions based solely on automated processing that produce legal effects concerning you or similarly significantly affect you (Art. 22 GDPR).

### **4d. Usage Data from Our Open-Source Packages**

When you run widgetbook_generator, it sends a usage report that includes the name of your package, a pseudonymous ID hashed from your Git email address, the SHA of your repository's first commit, the URL of the repository owner (for example github.com/acme) and the number of components and use cases. We do not try to link the hashed ID to a person. We remove user names from repository addresses and use the data at organization level only to understand which organizations use Widgetbook and to contact organizations that may benefit from Widgetbook Cloud.

**Opting out:** you can turn this off at any time. Add a `build.yaml` file next to your `pubspec.yaml` with:

```yaml
targets:
  $default:
    builders:
      widgetbook_generator:telemetry:
        enabled: false
```

Details on what is sent: [Telemetry](/telemetry).

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR. Our legitimate interest is improving our products and offering them to organizations that already use them. You can object at any time (see Section 10).

## **6. Cookies**

Our services use cookies. Cookies are small text files stored on your device that transmit certain information to the entity that sets the cookie. They serve to make our internet offering more user-friendly, efficient, and to facilitate navigation on our website.

This website uses the following types of cookies:

- Transient cookies (temporary use)
- Persistent cookies (time-limited use)

Transient cookies are automatically deleted when you close your browser. These include, in particular, session cookies. These store a randomly generated session ID that enables your browser’s requests to be assigned to the same session. This allows your device to be recognized upon return to our website.

Persistent cookies are automatically deleted after a predefined period, which can vary depending on the specific cookie.

You may, of course, use our website without accepting cookies. You can configure your browser settings as you wish, for example, to reject third-party cookies or all cookies, or to delete already stored cookies. Please note that if you choose not to accept cookies, our website may not function properly in certain areas.

To change your cookie preferences, please contact us at contact@widgetbook.io or use the button at the bottom of the website.

## **10. Rights of the Data Subject**

You have the right to obtain information about your personal data stored by us. Under statutory provisions, you also have the right to rectification of inaccurate data, restriction of processing, data portability, and erasure of your personal data. Please send such requests to contact@widgetbook.io with the subject “Data Protection”.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority if you believe the processing of your personal data infringes applicable data protection laws.

Where processing is based on your consent, you have the right to withdraw that consent at any time. The withdrawal does not affect the lawfulness of processing based on consent before its withdrawal.

You may also object at any time to the future processing of your personal data in accordance with legal requirements. In particular, you may object to processing for direct marketing purposes.

You can object at any time to the use of your data for sales outreach or direct marketing (Art. 21(2) GDPR), and to the analysis of your usage for onboarding suggestions or of your organization's open-source usage (Art. 21(1) GDPR). Reply to any of our messages or email contact@widgetbook.io. We will then stop using your data for these purposes.

## **11. Retention Period of Personal Data**

Unless otherwise stated in the specific sections above, the following applies: We store personal data for the duration of the relevant statutory retention periods or as long as the purpose of the data collection exists.

After expiry of the retention period, data is routinely deleted unless it is still required for contract initiation or performance. Where data is not deleted because it is required for other and legally permissible purposes, its processing will be restricted as far as possible. In such cases, the data will be blocked and not processed for other purposes. This particularly applies to data required to be retained for commercial or tax law reasons.

Information about companies (company name, publicly visible use of Flutter or Widgetbook, history of our contact) is kept as long as it is relevant to us, as it is not personal data. Personal data about prospects collected from public sources (Section 4a), such as names, job titles and profiles, is deleted after 24 months if there has been no further contact, or immediately after you object, unless legal retention obligations apply.

Usage analysis for onboarding (Section 4b) runs on the data of your active account and ends when your account is deleted. Notes derived from it, such as which help we offered you, are deleted 24 months after our last contact. Open-source usage data (Section 4d) is kept at organization level only.

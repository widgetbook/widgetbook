# Review a Change

A review happens in the browser.
Open the review, look at each changed screen, compare against the design, and leave a decision.
The developer is notified and responds.
Neither side runs the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the review">
    Every pull request gets a review automatically.
    Open the review from the link in the pull request, from an [email or Slack notification](/cloud/reviews/notifications/email), or from the project in Widgetbook Cloud.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at each changed story">
    A story is one component in one state (see the [Glossary](/cloud/for-designers/glossary)).
    The review lists every story that changed, **Baseline** on the left and **Latest** on the right.
    Click **Show diff** to shade what changed in pink, so even small shifts stand out.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/review-diff.png" zoom caption="Show diff shades every change in pink" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Interact live with the story">
    A snapshot is a still image.
    To feel the change, click **View Build** to open the story in Widgetbook.
    Play with different args and addon combinations to check the user experience, not just the pixels.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/view-build.png" zoom caption="View Build opens the story in Widgetbook" />

    <YouTube id="0YGbkE5qITE" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare against the Figma design">
    When the developer set a design link on the story, click **View in Figma** to open the matching component and compare against the build.
    See [Compare against Figma](/cloud/for-designers/figma) for how to keep this reliable.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/view-in-figma.png" zoom caption="Click View in Figma on the story" />
    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/figma-design.png" zoom caption="The matching component opens in the Figma file" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Comment on the specific change">
    Use the comment section on a story diff to tie feedback to that exact change.
    A comment on a story tells the developer which component and state to fix.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/comment.png" zoom caption="Comment on a story to point at the exact change" />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit a decision">
    Click the **Review changes** button in the top-right corner, pick an option, and click **Submit Review**:

    - **Approve.** Approve merging these changes.
    - **Comment.** Submit general feedback without explicit approval.
    - **Request changes.** Request changes before merging.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/submit-review.png" zoom />

    With [required reviewers](/cloud/reviews/required-reviewer) configured, a designer's approval can be what unblocks the merge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The developer responds">
    The decision shows on the pull request.
    After a request for changes, the developer revises and pushes again, which updates the review.
    Re-check the stories that changed and update the decision.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to look for

A review that reaches a designer should already contain only intentional changes.
The task is to judge whether those changes are right.

A snapshot is the only way to review a state.
A state missing from the diff is itself a finding, so ask for the scenario to be added.

### Confirm against the design

Compare each snapshot against Figma:

- **States.** Are empty, loading, error, selected, and disabled states all correct, not just the default?
- **Content extremes.** Does the layout hold with very long text, very short text, missing values, and large numbers?
- **Dark mode.** Does the change look right in light and dark, not just the one it was built in?
- **Text scaling.** Does the layout survive larger accessibility text sizes without clipping or overlap?
- **Spacing.** Is padding, margin, and alignment consistent with the design and with neighbours?
- **Colour and type.** Do fills, text colours, and type styles match the design exactly, with no near-misses?

### Accessibility

Contrast and visible focus states are clear from the image.
Semantics, labels, and focus order are not visible in a screenshot.
Confirm those in code review, or in Widgetbook Cloud's accessibility view when the project has it enabled.

# Doing a Review

A review happens in your browser. Open it, look at each changed screen, compare against the design, and leave your decision. The developer is notified and responds. Neither of you runs the app.

<Image theme="light" src="/assets/cloud/review.light.png" />
<Image theme="dark" src="/assets/cloud/review.dark.png" />

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the review">
    Every pull request gets a review automatically. Open it from the link in the
    pull request, from your [email or Slack notification](/cloud/reviews/notifications/email),
    or from the project in Widgetbook Cloud. Nothing to check out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at each changed story">
    A story is one component in one state (see the
    [Glossary](/cloud/for-designers/glossary)). The review lists every story that
    changed, old version on the left, new version on the right. Click **Show diff**
    to shade what changed in pink, so even small shifts stand out.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/show-diff.png" zoom />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare against the Figma design">
    If the developer set a design link on the story, a **View in Figma** button
    opens the matching component in Figma. See
    [Working with Figma](/cloud/for-designers/figma) for how this works and how to
    make it reliable.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/figma-review.png" zoom />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Comment on the specific change">
    Use the comment section on a story diff to tie feedback to that exact change,
    so the developer knows which component and state you mean.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/add-comment.png" zoom />

    Use **mark as viewed** to track what you have been through. If a story changes
    again later, its viewed state resets so you re-check it.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/mark-as-viewed.png" zoom />
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your decision">
    Click the **Review changes** button in the top-right corner and pick a status:

    - **Approved.** The changes are right and can merge.
    - **Comment.** Feedback without approving or blocking.
    - **Changes requested.** Something needs revising before it merges.

    <Image src="/assets/cloud/reviews/example.png" zoom />

    If your team uses [required reviewers](/cloud/reviews/required-reviewer), your
    approval as a designer can be what unblocks the merge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The developer responds">
    Your decision shows on the pull request. If you requested changes, the developer
    revises and pushes again. That updates the review. You re-check the stories that
    changed and update your decision.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to look for

A review that reaches you should already contain only intentional changes. Judge whether they are right:

- **States.** Are empty, loading, error, selected, and disabled states all correct, not just the default?
- **Content extremes.** Does it hold up with very long text, very short text, missing values, and large numbers?
- **Dark mode.** Does it look right in both light and dark, not just the one it was built in?
- **Text scaling.** Does the layout survive larger accessibility text sizes without clipping or overlap?
- **Spacing.** Is padding, margin, and alignment consistent with the design and with neighbours?
- **Token usage.** Do colours, typography, and spacing come from design tokens, not one-off values?
- **Design system reuse.** Does it reuse the right shared component instead of reinventing one?
- **Accessibility.** Are semantics, labels, and focus order correct, not just the pixels?

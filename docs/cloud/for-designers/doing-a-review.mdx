# Doing a Review

A review happens entirely in your browser. You open the review, look at each changed screen, compare it against the design, and leave your decision. The developer is notified and responds — all without either of you running the app.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the review">
    Every pull request gets a review automatically. Open it from the link in the
    pull request, from your [email or Slack notification](/cloud/reviews/notifications/email),
    or from the project in Widgetbook Cloud. You do not need to check anything out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Look at each changed story">
    A **story** is one component shown in one state (see the
    [Glossary](/cloud/for-designers/glossary)). The review lists every story that
    changed. For each one, the old version is on the left and the new version on
    the right. Click **Show diff** to highlight exactly what changed — differences
    are shaded in pink, so even small shifts are easy to spot.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Compare against the Figma design">
    If the developer set a design link on the story, a **View in Figma** button
    opens the matching component in Figma so you can compare the implementation
    against the source of truth. See [Working with Figma](/cloud/for-designers/figma)
    for how this works and how to make it more reliable.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Comment on the specific change">
    Use the comment section on a story diff to leave feedback tied to that exact
    change, so the developer knows precisely which component and state you mean.
    Use **mark as viewed** to track which changes you have already been through;
    if a story changes again later, its viewed state resets so you re-check it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit your decision">
    Click the **Review changes** button in the top-right corner to publish your
    review with one of three statuses:

    - **Approved** — the changes are right and can be merged.
    - **Comment** — you are leaving feedback without approving or blocking.
    - **Changes requested** — something needs to be revised before it merges.

    If your team uses [required reviewers](/cloud/reviews/required-reviewer), your
    approval as a designer may be what unblocks the merge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="The developer responds">
    Your decision is reflected on the pull request. If you requested changes, the
    developer revises and pushes again, which updates the review; you then re-check
    the stories that changed and update your decision.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## What to look for

A review that reaches you should already contain only intentional changes. Your job is to judge whether they are right. A practical checklist:

- **States.** Are empty, loading, error, selected, and disabled states all correct — not just the default one?
- **Content extremes.** Does it hold up with very long text, very short text, missing values, and large numbers?
- **Dark mode.** Does the change look right in both light and dark, not just the one the developer happened to build in?
- **Text scaling.** Does the layout survive larger accessibility text sizes without clipping or overlap?
- **Spacing.** Is padding, margin, and alignment consistent with the design and with neighbouring components?
- **Token usage.** Are colours, typography, and spacing coming from design tokens rather than one-off hard-coded values?
- **Design system reuse.** Is this reusing the right shared component, or quietly reinventing one that already exists?
- **Accessibility.** Are semantics, labels, and focus order correct — not just the pixels?
